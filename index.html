Who we are

Experienced, hands-on buyers of foil and laminate scrap.

If you manufacture or use packaging with aluminum foil, plastic film or paper laminates, we can supply secure, full-service recycling for your facility. In addition to buying aluminum foil laminate scrap, we are always in the market for all types of usable surplus packaging rolls.

Recycling is a key component in your sustainable packaging chain. By recycling your aluminum foil laminate scrap we can help you attain a zero-waste footprint for your manufacturing facility. Please give us a call — we can help.

What we recycle

Six material streams, one call

We can pick up anywhere in North America, or you can ship to our Ansonia facility. Mixed loads on the same trailer are fine.

Aluminum Foil & Laminates

Bare foil, lacquered foil, and plastic and paper laminates — rolls, coils, bales, cut sheets and edge trim.

Learn more

Metallized Film & Paper Rolls

Clean, dry, free-running metallized film and paper in roll form. Unused cold and hot stamping rolls accepted.

Learn more

Coated & Uncoated Paper Rolls

Unprinted coated and uncoated paper rolls, including silicone release, colored and poly-coated papers.

Learn more

Adhesive Backed Materials

Usable label stock and tape rolls in master and slit form — paper, BOPP, PVC, PE and fabric tapes.

Learn more

Plastic Film

Single-resin, unlaminated film rolls — PP, PET, LDPE, HDPE, PVC and nylon — plus many laminate films.

Learn more

Non-Woven Fabric

Spunlace, spunbond, meltblown, air-laid, wet-laid, needle-punched and medical gown laminates in roll form.

Surplus roll stock

We are also buyers of surplus roll stock.

In addition to our foil recycling program, we buy surplus roll stock. Let us know what you have, and we can arrange pick-up and fast payment for your unwanted aluminum foil inventory, paper rolls, nonwoven fabric, and laminate and plastic films.
