What we recycle

Plastic Film

We purchase single-resin, unlaminated plastic film in roll form.

Plastic films are an essential component in the food and merchandise packaging industries, and there are an infinite number of polymer film structures. Our main interest is in single-resin, unlaminated film rolls. Let us know if you have PP, PET, LDPE, HDPE, PVC or nylon film rolls you would like to move.

The material must be clean, dry and free-running. We can also use many types of laminate films. Just send us the details.

We can pick up anywhere in North America or you can ship to our Ansonia facility. Our minimum pick-up is 35,000 lbs. You can also deliver smaller amounts to our Connecticut location.
