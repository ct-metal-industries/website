What we recycle

Non-Woven Fabric

We purchase many types of non-woven fabric in roll form.

The material must be clean, dry and free-running. We are interested in surplus, seconds and overruns from a number of industries — let us know what you have.

We can pick up full truckloads anywhere in North America or you can ship directly to our Ansonia, Connecticut facility.

We currently purchase

  • Spunlace nonwoven fabric
  • Heat-sealing nonwoven fabric
  • Pulp air-laid nonwoven fabric
  • Wet laid nonwoven fabric
  • Spunbond nonwoven fabric
  • Meltblown nonwoven fabric
  • Needle punched nonwoven fabric
  • Medical gown laminate
  • Stitched nonwoven fabric

Key benefits of recycling

  • Lower your trash bill
  • Potential source of untapped revenue
  • ISO 14000 compliance
  • Obtain potential carbon credits
  • Increase your company's “green” profile
  • Provides an accurate audit of your waste stream
