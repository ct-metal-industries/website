What we recycle

Aluminum Foil & Laminates

We purchase bare foil, lacquered foil, and plastic and paper laminates.

We can accept usable rolls, coils, bales, cut sheets and edge trim, which can be mixed on the same trailer. We can provide certified destruction for sensitive material.

Foil laminates are recycled in an environmentally sound process that recovers the aluminum and converts the polymers to fuel the process.

We can pick up anywhere in North America or you can ship to our Ansonia facility. Our minimum pick-up is 10,000 lbs or more. You can also deliver smaller amounts to our Connecticut location.

Pricing

Foil scrap is valued on the recoverable metal content. Generally we pay for material with a metal content of 35% or better. The higher the metal content, the better the pricing. The same holds true for copper laminates. Send us a sample (one square foot is adequate) and we will evaluate it and call you with a price. Be sure to include a business card with your samples.
